## Philosophy

Nox is [AGPLv3](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.en.html) licensed software.
This license ensures that Nox remains free and open for everyone to use, modify, and re-distribute.
The commercial use of Nox is allowed under the terms of the AGPLv3 license.

The federated architecture of Nox promotes decentralization and resilience.
It's makes it possible for anyone to host their own Nox server,
fostering a diverse and vibrant ecosystem of interconnected worlds.

Moddable clients empower users to tailor their Nox experience to their preferences.
This flexibility encourages creativity and innovation within the Nox community.

## When to use Nox?

Nox is ideal for developers and creators who want to build 
and explore virtual worlds with a high degree of customization.
Whether you're creating social experiences, games, or educational environments, 
Nox provides the tools and infrastructure to bring your vision to life.
